Cuplock scaffolding is a modular system in which vertical standards and horizontal ledgers connect through cup-and-blade locking components. Component selection and bay arrangement should be based on the approved scaffolding design, site conditions and intended use.

Cuplock product components

Vertical standards

Vertical members form the main supporting framework. The cup positions, overall length, pipe dimensions and end details should match the project requirement.

Horizontal ledgers

Horizontal members connect standards and establish the bay length. Confirm the required blade-to-blade length and end connection details before ordering.

Related components

Base jacks, U-head jacks, base plates, braces and other accessories may be required depending on the designed scaffolding arrangement. Confirm availability when requesting a quotation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a vertical standard and a ledger?

The vertical standard is the upright member of the system. The ledger is a horizontal member that connects standards and establishes the bay length.

Can I order different ledger lengths?

Share the required blade-to-blade length and drawing. The exact specification and availability should be confirmed with our team.

Do these dimensions confirm the safe load capacity?

No. Component dimensions alone do not establish safe working load. A complete system assessment and project-specific design are required.

Can I send a BOQ for quotation?

Yes. Include your BOQ or drawing, component quantities, delivery location and purchase or rental preference.
