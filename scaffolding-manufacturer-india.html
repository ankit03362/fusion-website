How to specify your scaffolding requirement

To help us respond accurately, include the following details in your enquiry:

  • Product type: cuplock standards, ledgers, props or other scaffolding material.
  • Required dimensions, pipe diameter and wall thickness, where applicable.
  • Quantity and whether the requirement is for purchase or rental.
  • Project location, expected dispatch date and delivery arrangement.
  • Any drawings, bill of quantities or technical specification issued for the project.
Engineering and safety note: Scaffolding selection, allowable loads, bracing, foundations, ties and erection arrangements must be assessed for the actual site and design. Do not select a system or assume a safe working load from a product description alone. Follow the approved project design and applicable safety requirements.
