Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to rent or buy scaffolding?

It depends on how long you need the material and how often you will reuse it. Renting usually suits short or one-off projects, while buying can suit repeated use over a long period. Send your expected duration and quantity and we can quote the option you prefer.

What do you need to quote a rental?

The material list and quantities, project location, start date, expected rental period and any drawing or BOQ.

Do you supply outside Chhattisgarh?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from across India, and dispatch feasibility and freight are confirmed for each order.
